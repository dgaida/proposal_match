# Usage Guide

This guide provides an overview of how to use the Funding Research and Collaboration App.

## 1. Call Summarization  
- **Purpose**: Analyze research funding calls from URLs.  
- **How to use**:  
    1. Enter a research call URL in the input field.  
    2. Click **Analyze Call** (or **Ausschreibung analysieren**).  
    3. The app will fetch the content and provide an AI-generated summary and extracted metadata.  
    4. You can save the analysis for later or email it.  

## 2. FIT Search  
- **Purpose**: Search for calls in the FIT Uni Kassel database.  
- **How to use**:  
    1. Enter search terms (e.g., "KI", "Nachhaltigkeit").  
    2. Click **Search FIT**.  
    3. View the results and AI-generated summaries.  

## 3. Company Indexing  
- **Purpose**: Build a database of potential collaboration partners.  
- **How to use**:  
    1. Provide company URLs (manual input, text file, or local folder).  
    2. The app will crawl the websites and store metadata (SME status, industry, location) and semantic summaries.  

## 4. Matching & Hybrid Search  
- **Purpose**: Find the best partners for a specific call.  
- **How to use**:  
    1. Choose between **Automatic Matching** (based on a saved call) or **Manual Search**.  
    2. Apply filters for country, state, or organization type.  
    3. Click **Find Organizations**.  
    4. Use **Suggest Research Topics** to get concrete project ideas.  

## 5. LinkedIn Integration (Note)  
- **Notice**: This feature is currently restricted by LinkedIn's anti-scraping measures.  

## 6. Language Support  
- Toggle between **German** and **English** using the switcher at the top right of the application.  
- Note: AI-generated content (summaries, justifications) will remain in German.
